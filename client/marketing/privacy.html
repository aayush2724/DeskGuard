Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

In short

DeskGuard is built so that it can tell you whether a desk is free without knowing who is sitting at it. The live map and check-in flow work without accounts, cookies, cameras, or sensors. The only personal information DeskGuard stores is what you choose to send through the contact form.

What DeskGuard is

DeskGuard is a web application made up of a public website (this site), a live desk map and QR check-in app, a staff-only librarian dashboard, and an API that stores desk status. This policy covers all of them.

Information we store

Desk activity

When a desk is checked in, set to Away, released, confirmed with Still here?, or reset by staff, the API records the desk ID, its status, and timestamps, plus a short event message such as "Desk A-01 checked in". Nothing links these events to a person: there are no student accounts, names, device identifiers, or location data. Anyone can view the live map.

Contact-form submissions

If you use the contact form we store the name, email address, institution, approximate floor count, and message you enter, together with the time of submission, so that we can reply to you. We use this information only to respond to your enquiry.

Data kept in your own browser

  • Librarian access key. When staff sign in to the librarian dashboard, the access key is kept in that browser's local storage so they don't have to re-enter it, and is sent to the API with each staff request. Signing out removes it.
  • Desks you checked in from this browser. The live map keeps a list of desk IDs you checked in to, in local storage, so that "Still here?" prompts are only shown for your own desk. This list never leaves your browser.

Clearing your browser's site data removes both.

Technical and server data

Like any web service, our hosting providers process your IP address and basic request details (time, path, browser type) in standard server logs when you load the site or call the API. The API also keeps a short-lived, in-memory count of requests per IP address to limit abuse; this count is discarded within a minute and is never written to disk by DeskGuard.

Your camera

The QR scanner uses your device camera, with your permission, to read desk stickers. Decoding happens entirely in your browser. Camera images are never uploaded or stored, and permission is only requested when you press Start scanner. You can use the manual desk picker instead if you prefer not to grant camera access.

What we don't do

  • No cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking pixels.
  • No cameras, sensors, Bluetooth or Wi-Fi scanning in the library.
  • No third-party fonts or scripts: everything the site loads is served from this domain.
  • We don't sell or share the information above with anyone except the hosting providers needed to run the service.

Where data is processed

The website is hosted on Vercel and the API on Render. Desk status and contact submissions are stored in a PostgreSQL database and a Redis cache run by managed hosting providers on our behalf.

How long we keep it

  • Desk status is overwritten as desks change state.
  • Activity log entries (desk ID, status, timestamp, message) are currently kept until they are cleared by the operator. They contain no personal information.
  • Contact-form submissions are kept until your enquiry has been handled, or until you ask us to delete them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete any contact-form submission you've made. Depending on where you live, you may also have statutory rights regarding your personal information. To make a request, use the contact details below and tell us the email address you submitted with.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be noted in the changelog.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: use the contact form.
