How it works

From walk-in to seated
in seconds.

  1. 01

    Walk In

    Check the live map on your phone before you reach the floor. No app download, no account — free desks are already marked.

  2. 02

    Scan the QR

    Every desk has a QR sticker. Scan it with your phone camera to check in — that's the whole process.

  3. 03

    Map Updates

    The live floor plan marks the desk as occupied the moment you check in, for everyone who has the map open.

  4. 04

    Going Away?

    Stepping out for coffee? Tap Away. The desk is held for 20 minutes and flagged as abandoned if you don't come back.

  5. 05

    Seat Freed

    Abandoned desks are flagged for staff and can be claimed by the next student. After two hours a quick "Still here?" check keeps long sessions honest.

The Complete Setup

Hover over or focus the modules to see what powers DeskGuard.

  • LIVE MAP

    Live Map

    A real-time floor plan of every desk, pushed to every open browser the moment a status changes.

  • GHOST SEAT DETECTION

    Ghost Seat Detection

    Away timers and "Still here?" checks flag desks that were left behind — no sensors, no cameras, just time.

  • ANTI-HOARDING TIMER

    Anti-Hoarding Timer

    Desks on Away are held for a set time (20 minutes by default) and marked abandoned if nobody returns.

  • LIBRARIAN DASHBOARD

    Librarian Dashboard

    A key-protected control panel for staff to monitor zones, reset desks, print QR sheets, and read the activity log.

  • ACTIVITY LOG

    Activity Log

    Every check-in, away, release, and reset is recorded with a timestamp so staff can review what happened.

  • PRIVACY-FIRST

    Privacy-First

    No cameras, no sensors, no student accounts. DeskGuard stores desk IDs and timestamps — not people.
